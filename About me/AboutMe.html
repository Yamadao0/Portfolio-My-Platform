Motivational Letter

My name is Aslan Khabulov, and I am an aspiring software developer from Kazakhstan with a strong interest in science, technology, and creative problem-solving. Since childhood, I have been fascinated by how the world works and how its complexity can be understood, described, and explained. I have always been drawn to exact sciences, particularly mathematics, physics, and computer science. For me, programming, creating digital models, and working with computers have never been merely technical activities. I see them as a form of art. Just as a sculptor shapes stone into a sculpture or a mathematician solves a challenging problem, I find motivation in the process of creation itself: transforming an idea into something tangible, overcoming difficulties, and discovering solutions through my own efforts. I am currently a fourth-year student at the Eurasian Techno-Economic College in Kazakhstan, majoring in Software Development. Throughout my education, I have continued to explore the relationship between analytical thinking and creativity. Alongside programming, I have developed an interest in digital design and visual creation through applications such as Blender, Photoshop, and After Effects. These tools have allowed me to explore different ways of expressing ideas and have strengthened my appreciation for the combination of technical precision and artistic vision. Visual Studio Code has also become an important part of my working environment as I continue to develop my programming skills. My learning experience has taught me that engineering requires more than technical knowledge. Difficulties, including everyday challenges and problems that do not have immediate solutions, have helped me develop patience, discipline, and persistence. When something does not work as expected, I try to understand the underlying cause rather than simply hide the symptoms. I believe that this approach to problem-solving is essential for becoming a capable engineer and developing reliable software. One of the most valuable experiences in my academic development was my internship at Gamma Technologies, where I worked as a Software Developer Intern from June 3 to August 12, 2026. During this period, I participated in projects related to cryptographic information protection, secure data transmission, and the development of systems designed to protect communication channels over public networks. This experience gave me a clearer understanding of the importance of cybersecurity in modern computing and demonstrated how programming knowledge can be applied to practical technical challenges. In addition to my internship, I have developed my own portfolio website to showcase my work and demonstrate my growing technical skills. The website includes several of my personal projects and is available at [ https://yamadao0.github.io/Portfolio-My-Platform/ ]. Building my portfolio has encouraged me to learn independently, experiment with digital tools, and turn my ideas into practical results. Together, these experiences have strengthened my discipline, analytical thinking, and determination to pursue Computer Science and develop secure, reliable, and effective software systems. Computer Science attracts me because it brings together logical reasoning, creativity, and the opportunity to solve meaningful problems. Among its many fields, I am particularly interested in artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, and computer animation. Artificial intelligence fascinates me because it raises fundamental questions about intelligence, learning, and the possibilities of creating increasingly sophisticated computational systems. I would like to understand how these systems work and contribute to their development. Cybersecurity interests me because digital systems have become essential to modern society, making the protection of information, privacy, and critical infrastructure increasingly important. I respect the professionals who safeguard these systems and would like to contribute to making technology more secure and trustworthy. Computer animation appeals to my creative side because it combines technology, visual storytelling, and the ability to bring imagined worlds and characters to life. Although these fields differ in their applications, each offers an opportunity to combine technical expertise with creativity and continuous learning. My interest in China extends beyond its educational opportunities. I have long been fascinated by its rich history, cultural heritage, and mythology, which have inspired my imagination and encouraged me to learn more about the country. At the same time, I recognize China as an important center of technological development, engineering, and scientific innovation. Studying there would allow me to pursue my academic interests in a new cultural environment, gain an international perspective, and learn from a different educational and technological landscape. I see this experience as an opportunity to grow both academically and personally. My long-term goal in Computer Science is not simply to develop programs that function as intended, but to help build reliable, secure, and accessible systems that contribute to technological progress. I want to strengthen my theoretical foundations, develop my practical abilities, and explore the areas of computing that most closely match my interests. I understand that achieving these goals requires sustained effort, intellectual curiosity, and a willingness to learn from failure. I am prepared to dedicate my time and energy to my education, challenge myself academically, and continue developing as both a programmer and a future engineer. Thank you for considering my application. I would welcome the opportunity to pursue my academic goals, contribute my creativity and determination to the university community, and develop the knowledge and skills necessary to make a meaningful contribution to the field of Computer Science.
